# CFL Wash Co. Design Guide

This document covers brand personality and visual direction.

Implementation details, token values, component APIs, and responsive rules live in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Asset inventory and logo usage live in [BRAND_ASSETS.md](./BRAND_ASSETS.md).

## Brand Purpose

CFL Wash Co. is a premium local exterior cleaning brand for Central Florida homeowners who want cleaner driveways, sidewalks, walkways, and concrete without a complicated sales process.

The website should make three things clear:

- CFL Wash Co. is local to Central Florida.
- The launch offer is concrete-focused curb appeal cleaning.
- Requesting a quote is simple and direct.

## Visual Direction

The site should feel:

- Clean
- Bright
- Premium
- Local
- Easy to scan

The work is homeowner-friendly, not industrial or corporate. The logo already carries enough personality, so the rest of the site should stay restrained.

Preferred direction:

- Deep navy, water blue, warm gold, curb green, and warm neutral support colors
- White and soft off-white surfaces for the main page
- Cream and water-tinted section rhythm across public pages
- Branded CTA panels and action buttons driven by shared component variants
- Clear hierarchy over decoration
- Premium spacing and type rhythm
- Real photography when available

Avoid:

- Industrial black-and-yellow styling
- Overly dark layouts
- Excessive gradients
- Decorative novelty fonts
- Overly complex UI chrome

## UX Principles

- Make the quote path obvious at every key touchpoint.
- Keep the site fast to parse on mobile first.
- Prefer sentence-case headings and plain language.
- Keep paragraphs short and readable.
- Use supporting visual cues only when they improve clarity.
- Maintain strong keyboard focus states and accessible contrast.

## Homepage Philosophy

The homepage is a lead-generation page, not a brand campaign page.

It should prioritize:

- Concrete-focused services
- Clear service areas
- Simple request-quote actions
- Realistic local positioning
- Room for future proof photography

The homepage can get more polished over time, but it should not drift into a generic marketing landing page or a broad exterior-cleaning homepage that blurs the MVP offer. Interior public pages should share the same visual system through tokens, primitives, and page patterns rather than page-specific theme styling. The current homepage is the approved visual reference for the sitewide navy, cream, water, white, and gold rhythm.

## Stitch Rules

Use Stitch to explore polished layout ideas, spacing, and responsive composition.

Stitch should:

- Preserve the MVP service focus
- Use `CFL Wash Co.` as the primary wordmark text
- Keep the quote CTA visible in the header, hero, service sections, and final CTA
- Design reusable website sections, not a one-off mockup
- Leave room for real before/after photos
- Include mobile layout direction
- Keep copy claim-safe

Stitch should not:

- Create final logos
- Invent reviews, ratings, awards, insurance status, or licenses
- Make roof cleaning, house washing, fleet washing, or commercial work the primary offer
- Design a booking portal, payment flow, dashboard, or marketplace

## Claim-Safe Design Guidance

The interface should never imply capabilities that have not been verified.

Do not design or present:

- Ratings or star badges
- Awards, medals, certificates, or shield icons as proof
- Guarantees
- Insurance or licensing claims
- Same-day service or instant booking
- Fake before/after proof
- Inventory or workflows that do not exist yet

When in doubt, keep the design factual and simple.
