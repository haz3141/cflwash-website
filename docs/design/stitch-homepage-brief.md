# CFL Wash Co. Stitch Design Brief

## Project

CFL Wash Co. is a Central Florida exterior cleaning and pressure washing company.

Domain:
cflwash.com

Tagline:
Exterior cleaning made easy across Central Florida.

Current site stack:
Astro 6, Tailwind 4, Cloudflare Pages.

## Design goal

Create a premium but approachable local service website that feels clean, trustworthy, bright, fast, and easy to book.

The design should feel like a modern Central Florida exterior cleaning company, not a generic industrial pressure washing site.

The site should help homeowners quickly understand:

- What CFL Wash Co. does
- Where it serves
- Why it is trustworthy
- How to request a quote
- That the company specializes first in driveway, sidewalk, walkway, and concrete cleaning

## Primary audience

Homeowners in Central Florida, especially:

- Deltona
- Orange City
- DeBary
- DeLand
- Sanford
- Lake Mary

Primary use cases:

- HOA notice cleanup
- Dirty driveway or sidewalk
- Curb appeal improvement
- Preparing for guests, listing photos, or property sale
- General exterior refresh

## Services to highlight

Primary:

- Driveway pressure washing
- Sidewalk cleaning
- Walkway cleaning
- Concrete cleaning

Secondary/future:

- Patio cleaning
- Pool deck cleaning
- HOA notice cleanup
- Commercial storefront or concrete cleaning

Do not make the design feel like a roof cleaning or full-house soft wash company yet. Those can be future expansion services.

## Brand feel

Keywords:

- Clean
- Local
- Trustworthy
- Bright
- Simple
- Professional
- Friendly
- Florida
- Curb appeal
- Easy quote
- Before/after proof

Avoid:

- Dark industrial design
- Harsh black/yellow construction look
- Generic janitorial look
- Overly corporate tech startup look
- Overly beachy/tourist look
- Cluttered layouts

## Visual direction

Use a bright Florida daylight feeling.

Suggested colors:

- Clean white / soft off-white backgrounds
- Deep navy or slate for text
- Fresh blue accents for water/cleaning
- Subtle green accents for curb appeal/lawn/exterior freshness
- Soft warm neutral tones for Florida light and concrete

The design should have:

- Strong whitespace
- Clear section separation
- Rounded cards/buttons
- Trustworthy but not boring CTA buttons
- Mobile-first layout
- Service cards
- Simple process section
- Before/after proof area, even if placeholder for now

## Homepage sections

1. Header
   - Logo/text: CFL Wash Co.
   - Navigation:
     - Services
     - Service Areas
     - Request Quote
   - CTA:
     - Request Quote
     - Call button using placeholder phone

2. Hero
   Headline direction:
   Driveway, Sidewalk, and Exterior Cleaning in Central Florida

   Supporting copy direction:
   Exterior cleaning made easy across Central Florida. Request a simple quote for driveway, walkway, sidewalk, and concrete cleaning.

   CTAs:
   - Request a Quote
   - Call verified phone number when available

3. Trust bar
   Examples:
   - Local Central Florida service
   - HOA notice cleanup
   - Driveways, sidewalks, and concrete
   - Before/after proof coming soon

4. Core services
   Cards for:
   - Driveway Pressure Washing
   - Sidewalk & Walkway Cleaning
   - Concrete Cleaning

5. HOA / curb appeal feature
   Focus:
   Driveway + walkway + sidewalk cleaning for HOA notices and curb appeal.

6. Before/after proof placeholder
   Design a section where project images can go later.
   Do not rely on actual images yet.

7. Service areas
   Highlight:
   Deltona, Orange City, DeBary, DeLand, Sanford, Lake Mary.

8. Three-step process
   - Request a quote
   - Confirm the details
   - Get cleaned and receive before/after photos

9. FAQ section
   Keep it short and homeowner-friendly.

10. Final CTA
    Encourage quote request.

## Additional pages to design

Also provide direction for:

- Service page layout
- Service-area page layout
- Request quote page layout
- Mobile homepage layout

## Implementation notes

The design will be implemented in Astro + Tailwind.

Keep components reusable:

- Header
- Footer
- Button
- Section
- ServiceCard
- CTA

Do not design an app dashboard, customer portal, scheduling system, payment flow, or marketplace.

This is a local service lead-generation website first.
