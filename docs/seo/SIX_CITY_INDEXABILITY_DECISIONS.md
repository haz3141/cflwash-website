## Final Mechanics

- All six city pages use normal `index, follow` output from the shared layout.
- All six city pages remain included in the generated sitemap.
- `robots.txt` continues to reference `https://cflwash.com/sitemap-index.xml` and does not block pages that rely on page-level robots directives.
- Homepage, active service pages, header/footer navigation, and nearby-area links continue to promote only the approved six-city set.
- No city-service route matrix was created.
- No additional cities were added.

## Remaining Post-Launch Limitations

- Publishable project proof, testimonials, reviews, ratings, and before/after images still require real completed work, permission records, and separate implementation.
- Named HOA guidance remains excluded until supported by verified homeowner-safe evidence and owner operating policy.
- Edge-address wording for unincorporated Volusia, Greater DeLand/North DeLand, Blue Spring-area, Midway, airport-adjacent, Heathrow-area, county-pocket, and other mailing-name cases requires separate owner confirmation before publication.
- Additional Seminole and Volusia city pages require separate research and implementation issues after launch evidence supports them.
