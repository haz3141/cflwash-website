## 3. Residential and surface context

- `VERIFIED`: Sanford's Historic Preservation program identifies three National Register districts relevant to residential and homeowner page language: the Downtown Commercial Historic District, Sanford Residential Historic District, and Georgetown Historic District.
- `VERIFIED`: Those districts include homes, churches, schools, commercial buildings, and public buildings across multiple architectural styles, which supports more varied frontage, driveway, walk, and entry conditions than a newer subdivision-only page.
- `VERIFIED`: Sanford's Comprehensive Plan describes a Waterfront/Downtown Business District shaped by proximity to the St. Johns River and Lake Monroe and intended to preserve historic character while guiding redevelopment and infill.
- `VERIFIED`: Sanford RiverWalk is a nearly five-mile multi-use trail on Lake Monroe with lighting, landscaping, seawalls, and other waterfront infrastructure, which supports quote language around visible waterfront-adjacent concrete and drainage awareness without implying shoreline service work.
- `VERIFIED`: Sanford Public Works lists Street Division responsibilities that include pothole repairs, alley maintenance, litter and weed control, traffic control services, and sidewalk and curb reconstruction.
- `VERIFIED`: Sanford Public Works lists Stormwater Division responsibilities that include drainage construction and repairs, street sweeping, and drainage-structure cleaning and maintenance.
- `VERIFIED`: The active 2nd Street streetscape project documents reconstructed ADA sidewalks, curb and gutter work, driveway and parking reconstruction, brick-paver installation, lighting work, and alley/detour coordination in downtown Sanford.

### Surface implications for future page language
