# CFL Wash Co. Launch Plan

## 30-Day Launch Plan

### Days 1-7: Foundation

- [ ] Confirm business name, operating entity, and contact details.
- [ ] Replace placeholder phone number in `src/data/site.ts`.
- [ ] Confirm production domain setup for `cflwash.com`.
- [ ] Verify Cloudflare Pages production deployment.
- [ ] Finalize MVP service list: driveway, sidewalk, walkway, concrete, HOA notice cleanup, curb appeal cleaning.
- [ ] Confirm primary service areas: Deltona, Orange City, DeBary, DeLand, Sanford, Lake Mary.
- [ ] Add a simple working quote form or clear phone/email quote path.
- [ ] Review all website copy against `docs/strategy/CLAIMS.md`.

### Days 8-14: Local Presence

- [ ] Create or claim Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Add accurate business name, phone, website, hours, and service areas.
- [ ] Add real business photos only.
- [ ] Prepare first social/profile graphics using the approved brand direction.
- [ ] Build launch photo library: equipment, clean concrete, signs, shirts, owner/operator, process shots.
- [ ] Create repeatable before/after photo workflow.

### Days 15-21: First Jobs And Proof

- [ ] Complete first jobs in primary service areas.
- [ ] Capture before/after photos with customer permission.
- [ ] Record service type, city, surface, date, and notes for each job.
- [ ] Ask customers for honest Google reviews after completion.
- [ ] Add real project proof to the website only after permission is confirmed.
- [ ] Create first project pages once photos and job details are available.

### Days 22-30: Improve And Publish

- [ ] Publish full homepage content.
- [ ] Expand service-area pages only where there is real coverage intent.
- [ ] Add FAQ content based on actual customer questions.
- [ ] Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
- [ ] Check analytics, form tracking, call tracking, and conversion paths.
- [ ] Review mobile layout, page speed, forms, and all CTAs.
- [ ] Plan the next 30 days from actual lead and job data.

## Google Business Profile Checklist

- [ ] Use exact brand name: `CFL Wash Co.`
- [ ] Use the correct domain: `https://cflwash.com`.
- [ ] Use verified phone and email.
- [ ] Set service-area coverage accurately.
- [ ] Choose categories related to pressure washing/exterior cleaning.
- [ ] Add services matching the MVP offer.
- [ ] Add real photos, not stock photos.
- [ ] Add hours only after operating availability is confirmed.
- [ ] Do not add fake reviews or ask friends/family for reviews if they were not customers.
- [ ] Respond professionally to every review.

## First Jobs, Reviews, And Photo-Proof Checklist

- [ ] Capture before photos before wetting the surface.
- [ ] Capture after photos from the same angle when dry enough to show the result.
- [ ] Get permission before publishing customer property photos.
- [ ] Record city and service type for each job.
- [ ] Ask for honest reviews after the customer has inspected the work.
- [ ] Do not write reviews for customers.
- [ ] Do not edit photos in a misleading way.
- [ ] Add project proof gradually as real jobs accumulate.

## Tracking Checklist

- [ ] Install analytics.
- [ ] Set up Google Search Console.
- [ ] Track quote form submissions.
- [ ] Track phone CTA clicks.
- [ ] Track email CTA clicks.
- [ ] Track Google Business Profile leads if available.
- [ ] Track page-level conversions by service and area.
- [ ] Keep a simple lead source log during the first month.

## What Not To Overbuild Yet

- Online scheduling system
- Customer portal
- Payments
- Crew dispatch tools
- Complex CRM
- Full commercial/fleet washing section
- Roof cleaning or house washing as primary site architecture
- Dozens of thin city pages
- Final logo package before brand direction is validated
- Large blog content library before core service and project pages exist
