# CFL Wash Co. Claims Policy

## Safe Claims We Can Use Now

Use these only when they match the current offer and operational reality:

- CFL Wash Co. is a Central Florida exterior cleaning and pressure washing company.
- CFL Wash Co. serves Central Florida homeowners.
- Core services include driveway pressure washing, sidewalk cleaning, walkway cleaning, and concrete cleaning.
- Primary service areas include Deltona, Orange City, DeBary, DeLand, Sanford, and Lake Mary.
- CFL Wash Co. helps with curb appeal cleaning.
- CFL Wash Co. can help clean concrete surfaces affected by everyday buildup, grime, and staining.
- Requesting a quote is the primary next step.
- Before/after proof can be added as real projects are completed and approved for publication.

Preferred wording:

- `Request a quote`
- `Exterior cleaning made easy across Central Florida`
- `Driveway, sidewalk, walkway, and concrete cleaning`
- `HOA notice cleanup support`
- `Curb appeal cleaning for concrete surfaces`

## Claims Requiring Verification First

Do not use these until documentation or operational proof exists:

- Fully insured
- Licensed
- Bonded
- 5-star rated
- Top-rated
- Best pressure washing company
- Same-day service
- Instant booking
- Online scheduling
- Online payment
- 24/7 service
- Guaranteed results
- Satisfaction guaranteed
- Eco-friendly
- Environmentally safe
- Soft wash specialist
- Roof cleaning specialist
- Commercial fleet washing specialist
- Any exact years of experience
- Any customer count or job count
- Any award, certification, association, or accreditation

## Claims To Avoid

Avoid these even if they sound common in the industry:

- Fake or unverifiable reviews
- Fake star ratings
- `#1 pressure washing company`
- `Best in Central Florida`
- `Permanent stain removal`
- `Like new` as a guaranteed result
- `No damage guaranteed`
- `Chemical-free` unless the process truly uses no chemicals
- `Safe for all surfaces`
- Any claim that implies roof cleaning or house washing is the primary offer
- Any claim that implies commercial/fleet work is the MVP focus

## Review And Testimonial Policy

- Do not invent reviews.
- Do not write customer reviews for customers.
- Do not imply a rating until it exists on a public review platform.
- Do not use `5-star rated` until verified.
- Use only real testimonials from real customers.
- Get permission before publishing a testimonial on the website.
- Keep customer names limited to first name/last initial unless broader permission is given.
- Link or reference the source platform when practical.
- Remove or update review claims if the public rating changes.

## Insurance And Licensing Language Policy

Insurance:

- Do not claim `fully insured` until insurance status is confirmed.
- After confirmation, use exact language that matches the policy.
- Keep proof available internally before adding the claim to website copy, schema, or Google Business Profile.

Licensing:

- Do not claim `licensed` until licensing requirements and status are confirmed.
- If no specific pressure washing license is required in a service area, do not turn that into a misleading claim.
- Use precise language such as `registered business` only if true and verified.

## Before/After Photo Policy

- Use real CFL Wash Co. project photos only.
- Get customer permission before publishing private property photos.
- Avoid showing addresses, house numbers, license plates, people, children, and private details.
- Capture before and after photos from the same angle when possible.
- Do not over-edit photos to exaggerate results.
- Label photos clearly if surfaces are still wet, partly dry, or photographed under different lighting.
- Do not use stock photos as project proof.

## Environmental And Runoff Wording Policy

Environmental wording must be precise and verified.

Safe wording:

- `We use a controlled cleaning process.`
- `We review the surface and surrounding area before cleaning.`
- `Runoff considerations are handled according to the job conditions and applicable local requirements.`

Avoid unless verified:

- `Eco-friendly`
- `Environmentally safe`
- `Chemical-free`
- `Biodegradable`
- `Safe for all plants`
- `No runoff impact`
- `EPA approved`

If future environmental claims are added, verify the actual products, process, local requirements, and disposal/runoff handling before publishing.
