# ADR 0003: Separate Technical SEO From Content SEO

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The site needs crawlable, indexable pages now, but it does not yet have enough real-world proof for heavy content expansion.

## Decision

Treat technical SEO and content SEO as separate workstreams.

## Consequences

- Technical SEO can be completed without waiting for copy expansion
- Content work can be added later with real proof and FAQs
- Indexing, canonicals, and sitemap rules stay independent from editorial work
- The site can avoid thin or fake content while still being search-ready
